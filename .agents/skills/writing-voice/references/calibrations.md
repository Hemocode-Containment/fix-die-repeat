# Confirmed direct calibrations

These rules record choices confirmed directly by the manager. They are not corpus-derived
findings. Apply repository requirements first, then the most specific confirmed calibration, then
the corpus-derived profile.

New entries use this structure:

```markdown
## Artifact or register

Rule: State the confirmed writing constraint.

Representative sample: Preserve the writing pattern with neutral placeholders.

Confirmed: `YYYY-MM-DD`
```

## Pull-request bodies

Rule: Do not divide a pull-request body into heading blocks or a sectioned template. State what
changed and why directly, while preserving required issue-closing references and repository-
mandated disclosures.

Representative sample: Not recorded for this legacy direct calibration. Do not invent one.

Confirmed: `2026-07-25`
